deepagents is the LangChain library that packages production [agent](/agents/agent.md) patterns — planning, [subagents](/agents/subagents.md), durable [memory](/agents/memory.md), human-in-the-loop, and [workflows](/agents/workflows.md) — on top of LangGraph, which serves as its [harness](/agents/harness.md).
