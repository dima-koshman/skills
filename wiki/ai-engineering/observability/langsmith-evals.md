# LangSmith Evals

Dataset and evaluator tooling in LangSmith for offline and online agent evaluation.

Reference: <https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langchain/test/evals>

[LangSmith](langsmith.md)'s evaluation tooling runs datasets, LLM-as-judge and custom evaluators, and experiments over traces — the operational side of evals in this stack.
