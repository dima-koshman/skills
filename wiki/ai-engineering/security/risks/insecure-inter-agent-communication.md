# Insecure Inter-Agent Communication

Trust, spoofing, or tampering weaknesses in messaging between agents.

Reference: <https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026/>

In multi-agent systems, the channels between agents become an attack surface: unauthenticated messages, spoofed peers, and tampered hand-offs.
