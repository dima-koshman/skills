# Agentic Supply Chain Compromise

Malicious or compromised components — tools, MCP servers, models, dependencies — enter the agent supply chain.

Reference: <https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026/>

Supply chain compromise is the poisoning of what the agent depends on: a backdoored MCP server, a tampered model, a malicious package, or a spoofed tool.
