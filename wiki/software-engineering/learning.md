Practical labs and structured training for Linux, Kubernetes, and software infrastructure.

## LabEx

Browser-based hands-on labs and structured skill trees for Linux, DevOps, cybersecurity, and programming.

**Resource:** <https://labex.io/>
**Note date:** 2026-09-30

Reference: <https://labex.io/>

Note dated: 2026-09-30 18:00:00+00:00.

LabEx provides interactive online environments, guided labs, skill trees, and
projects. Use it for repeated practical exercises: shell commands, Linux
administration, containers, and Kubernetes. Its Linux and DevOps tracks are
useful companions to the structured
[Linux Foundation Kubernetes introduction](/software-engineering/learning.md#linux-foundation-training).

Prefer a concrete practice target—inspect a process, debug connectivity, or
deploy and inspect a workload—then repeat it without the walkthrough. A
successful guided lab is practice evidence, not by itself evidence of
production operating experience or certification readiness.

## Linux Foundation Training

Structured open-source training, including Introduction to Kubernetes (LFS158) and its hands-on exercises.

**Resource:** <https://training.linuxfoundation.org/>
**Note date:** 2026-09-30

Reference: <https://training.linuxfoundation.org/>

Note dated: 2026-09-30 18:00:00+00:00.

Linux Foundation Training provides a structured learning route for Linux and
cloud-native technologies. **Introduction to Kubernetes (LFS158)** is a free,
self-paced starting point for understanding Kubernetes architecture, components,
cluster access with Minikube, and deploying and accessing applications.
The public course page includes hands-on labs and assignments; some Linux
administration knowledge is helpful.

Connect its architecture material to the
[data, control, and management planes](/software-engineering/infrastructure/planes.md). Use
[LabEx](/software-engineering/learning.md#labex) for additional exercises, and study the
[service mesh](/software-engineering/infrastructure/service-mesh.md) after the underlying workload
and networking model is clear.
