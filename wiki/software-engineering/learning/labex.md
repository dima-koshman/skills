# LabEx

Browser-based hands-on labs and structured skill trees for Linux, DevOps, cybersecurity, and programming.

Reference: <https://labex.io/>

Note dated: 2026-09-30 18:00:00+00:00.

LabEx provides interactive online environments, guided labs, skill trees, and
projects. Use it for repeated practical exercises: shell commands, Linux
administration, containers, and Kubernetes. Its Linux and DevOps tracks are
useful companions to the structured
[Linux Foundation Kubernetes introduction](linux-foundation.md).

Prefer a concrete practice target—inspect a process, debug connectivity, or
deploy and inspect a workload—then repeat it without the walkthrough. A
successful guided lab is practice evidence, not by itself evidence of
production operating experience or certification readiness.
