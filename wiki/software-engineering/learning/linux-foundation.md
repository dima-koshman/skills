Linux Foundation Training provides a structured learning route for Linux and
cloud-native technologies. **Introduction to Kubernetes (LFS158)** is a free,
self-paced starting point for understanding Kubernetes architecture, components,
cluster access with Minikube, and deploying and accessing applications.
The public course page includes hands-on labs and assignments; some Linux
administration knowledge is helpful.

Connect its architecture material to the
[data, control, and management planes](/software-engineering/infrastructure/planes.md). Use
[LabEx](/software-engineering/learning/labex.md) for additional exercises, and study the
[service mesh](/software-engineering/infrastructure/service-mesh.md) after the underlying workload
and networking model is clear.
