## Topics

- [Metastable Failures](/software-engineering/reliability/metastable-failures.md) — Outages that persist after their trigger is gone, because the degraded system does extra work that keeps it overloaded.
- [Simplicity](/software-engineering/reliability/simplicity.md) — Reduce accidental complexity to make production systems easier to understand, change, and operate reliably.
- [Site Reliability Engineering](/software-engineering/reliability/sre.md) — Google's discipline of running production with software engineering: explicit reliability targets, an error budget that balances them against change, and automation in place of manual operations.
- [Toil](/software-engineering/reliability/toil.md) — Manual, repetitive operational work that scales with the service — capped and engineered away so a team's effort grows sublinearly with what it runs.
